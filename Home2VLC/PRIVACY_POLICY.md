# Privacy Policy for Home2VLC

**Effective Date: October 11, 2025**

Home2VLC is designed with your privacy as a top priority. This privacy policy explains how we handle information when you use our tvOS application.

## DATA COLLECTION

Home2VLC does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. All data processing occurs locally on your Apple TV device.

The following information is stored locally on your device only:
- Media URLs you enter or stream
- Application settings (port configuration, history length preferences)
- URL playback history (configurable, 1-50 items)

## DATA USAGE

The information stored on your device is used solely to:
- Enable media playback through VLC Media Player
- Provide quick access to recently played URLs
- Maintain your preferred application settings
- Facilitate the built-in HTTP API server for remote control

## NO DATA SHARING

Home2VLC does not share, sell, or transmit any data to third parties. No analytics, tracking, or advertising services are integrated into this application.

## NO EXTERNAL SERVERS

The application operates entirely on your local network. The optional HTTP server feature runs locally on your Apple TV and does not communicate with any external servers operated by us or third parties.

## NETWORK CONNECTIVITY

When you use the remote control API feature, your Apple TV accepts connections from devices on your local network. These connections are initiated by you and remain within your home network.

## THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

Home2VLC sends media URLs to VLC Media Player for playback. VLC Media Player is a separate application with its own privacy practices. Please review VLC's privacy policy for information about how it handles media streams.

## DATA RETENTION

URL history and settings are stored on your device until you:
- Manually clear the history through the app settings
- Adjust the history length setting (which removes older entries)
- Delete the Home2VLC application from your Apple TV

## USER CONTROL

You have complete control over your data:
- Clear URL history at any time through app settings
- Configure history length (1-50 items) or disable history tracking
- Adjust or disable the HTTP server feature
- Delete the app to remove all stored data

## CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Home2VLC does not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years of age. The application does not distinguish between users of different ages.

## OPEN SOURCE

Home2VLC is open source software released under the MIT License. The source code is available for inspection to verify our privacy practices.

## CHANGES TO THIS POLICY

We may update this privacy policy as we improve the application. Any changes will be reflected in app updates with a new effective date. Continued use of the application after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## CONTACT

For questions about this privacy policy or Home2VLC's privacy practices, please contact us through the app's GitHub repository or the support contact provided in App Store Connect.

---

This privacy policy applies specifically to the Home2VLC tvOS application and does not cover any third-party applications, services, or websites that may be accessed through media URLs you choose to stream.
